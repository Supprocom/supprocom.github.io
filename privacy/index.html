02 / what data, and why

Membership and license

  • Membership recordsYour Patreon name, tier, amounts and payment dates, as Patreon reports them. Used to know who holds a license and which months were paid. Legal basis: the contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) and accounting law (Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • Business detailsThe legal name of the business a membership is for, and a contact email, when you give them or we ask. Used to know which business the membership licenses. Legal basis: the contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Proof of categoryFor example a business-register extract, and the categories of your clients. Used only to check that the right tier is paid. Legal basis: legitimate interest in enforcing the license (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • GitHub sponsorshipsYour GitHub account name, amounts and dates, as GitHub reports them, if you sponsor mkn8rn on GitHub. A sponsorship is a donation and gives no license. Used to keep accounting records. Legal basis: accounting law (Art. 6(1)(c)).

Messages and reports

  • MessagesWhat you write to us. Used to answer you. Legal basis: the contract or legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f)), depending on the subject.
  • Vulnerability reportsYour email address, name if given, and the report. Used to fix the vulnerability, credit you if you wish, and report it to the authorities where the EU Cyber Resilience Act requires. Legal basis: that legal obligation and legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(c) and (f)).

Most licensees are businesses. This notice covers the personal data within that: names, sole traders' details and contact persons. The data comes from you, from Patreon and from GitHub.

03 / who receives it

Recipients

  • Patreon and GitHubPatreon runs the memberships and GitHub runs GitHub Sponsors, each as a separate controller under its own privacy policy. They send us the records above.
  • Accountant and tax authorityThe publisher's accountant, as a processor, and the Croatian Tax Administration where the law requires.
  • Security authoritiesCSIRT Croatia and ENISA, for vulnerabilities the Cyber Resilience Act requires to be reported.
  • Courts and lawyersOnly if a dispute about the license arises.

Never

  • Not soldYour data is not sold or shared for marketing.
  • No advertising or profilingIt is not used for advertising or for automated decisions.

05 / your rights

What can I ask for?

Access to your data, its correction or deletion, restriction of its processing, and a portable copy. You may object to processing based on legitimate interest.

How do I ask?

Email supprocom@mkn8rn.com. You may also complain to the Croatian data protection authority, AZOP (azop.hr).

Do I have to give my details?

Only what Patreon asks for to start a membership, and, if we ask, the legal name of the business it is for. Without it, we can't confirm your license.
