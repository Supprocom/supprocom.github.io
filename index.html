01 / licensing

Your choice of two licenses

  • AGPL-3.0 →The GNU Affero General Public License v3.0, for everyone.
  • Supprocom Commercial License →For businesses only. It lets you use the projects in closed-source products and services without the AGPL-3.0's obligations.

02 / commercial license FAQ

These answers are about the commercial license. Under the AGPL-3.0 you can do whatever you want with the libraries, as long as you follow its terms, such as sharing your source code.

Do I have to publish my source code or follow any of the AGPL-3.0 terms if I'm a member?

No; that is what the commercial license is for, while your business holds a membership at its tier.

Does my client need its own license if I have one?

No. Your membership covers what you deliver to them. One rule: if the client would pay EUR 100 itself, you need the EUR 100 tier. If the client later wants to change the Supprocom code itself, or pass it on, it needs its own membership, or can do so under the AGPL-3.0.

I'm a contractor for a company that's already a member. Do I need my own?

No. While you work for them, their license covers you.

Do I need one license per project, developer or product?

No. One membership covers every Supprocom project, everyone who works for your business, including contractors, and all your products. Each separate company needs its own.

What happens to software I already delivered if I end my membership?

Nothing. Copies delivered while you were a member stay licensed for your clients and users, as delivered.

Can I keep using the libraries after I stop?

Yes, for versions you have earned: once you have paid 12 months in a row after a version's release, that version is yours to keep for good. For anything else, the AGPL-3.0 applies again.

My one-person business hired someone. What now?

Move to the EUR 100 tier from the next month.

Do I get invoices?

Yes, from Patreon. Patreon sells the membership, so your invoice or receipt comes from Patreon, with any VAT on it.

Does a GitHub sponsorship give me the license?

No. Sponsoring mkn8rn on GitHub is a donation. Only a Patreon membership gives the license.

Can I use AI coding assistants on code that uses these libraries?

Yes. Just take reasonable steps so the provider doesn't train on your code, such as turning training off in its settings or using its business plan. What you can't do under the commercial license is use the libraries themselves to train AI models; under the AGPL-3.0 you can.

What can't I do?

Sell or license the libraries on their own, or train AI models on them, unless you're doing so under the AGPL-3.0 terms. Under either license, keep the copyright notices. Building and selling your own products and services with them is fine.

Do I get security fixes?

Yes, free, for five years from each version's release, while Supprocom is active and the business is still active. The end date is in each release's notes.

I'm using it for a hobby project. Do I need this?

No. Use it under the AGPL-3.0 for free.

The full terms are in the COMMERCIAL-LICENSE file of each repository; the files in each repository control. Third-party components keep their own licenses.
