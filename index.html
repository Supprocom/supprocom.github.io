01 / licensing

Your choice of two licenses

  • AGPL-3.0 →The GNU Affero General Public License v3.0, for everyone.
  • Supprocom Commercial License →For businesses only. It lets you use the projects in closed-source products and services without the AGPL-3.0's obligations.

02 / commercial license FAQ

These answers are about the commercial license. Under the AGPL-3.0 you can do whatever you want with the libraries, as long as you follow its terms, such as sharing your source code.

Do I have to publish my source code or follow any of the AGPL-3.0 terms if I'm a member?

No. While your business holds a membership at its tier, the commercial license applies instead.

Does my client need its own license if I have one?

No, your membership covers what you deliver to them, but if the client would pay EUR 100 itself, you need the EUR 100 tier. If the client later changes or passes on the Supprocom code itself, it needs its own membership, or can use the code under the AGPL-3.0.

I'm a contractor for a company that's already a member. Do I need my own?

No, while you work for them, their license covers what you build and deliver for them. For other clients or your own products, you need your own membership or the AGPL-3.0.

Do I need one license per project, developer or product?

No, one membership covers every Supprocom project, everyone who works for your business and all your products. Each separate company needs its own.

What happens to software I already delivered if I end my membership?

Nothing. Copies delivered while you were a member stay licensed for your clients and users, as delivered.

Can I keep using the libraries after I stop?

Yes: after 12 paid months in a row, you keep the versions released before those 12 months began. For anything else, the AGPL-3.0 applies again.

My one-person business hired someone. What now?

Move to the EUR 100 tier from the next month. The EUR 1 tier is only for a business where you are the only person working.

Do I get invoices?

Yes, from Patreon. Patreon sells the membership, so it issues your invoice or receipt, with any VAT on it.

Does a GitHub sponsorship give me the license?

No, sponsoring mkn8rn on GitHub is a donation. Only a Patreon membership gives the license.

Can I use AI coding assistants on code that uses these libraries?

Yes, if you take reasonable steps so the provider doesn't train on your code, such as turning training off. Only training AI models on the libraries themselves is ruled out under the commercial license.

What can't I do?

Under the commercial license, you can't sell or license the libraries on their own or train AI models on them, and under either license you keep the copyright notices. Building and selling your own products with them is fine.

Do I get security fixes?

Yes, free for five years from each version's release, while Supprocom and its business stay active. Each release's notes give the end date.

I'm using it for a hobby project. Do I need this?

No, you can use it for free under the AGPL-3.0, as long as you follow its terms. The commercial license is only for businesses that don't want to follow them.

The full terms are in the COMMERCIAL-LICENSE file of each repository; the files in each repository control. Third-party components keep their own licenses.
